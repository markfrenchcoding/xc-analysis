Every number on the Program tab is computed over a population you can see and change, because a statistic taken over this list is a statistic about the people who stayed.

Four-year development

Mean change in VDOT from one grade to the next. The same calculation over three different groups of people. Nothing else changes.

Who stays

Of the athletes who raced here as freshmen, how many were still racing as seniors. Late entries are not in this fraction: somebody who joined in grade 11 is not a dropout from the class they graduate with.

Who is here

Athletes with a recorded race, by school year and grade. Depth is not the same thing as a top five.

Front five, and the five behind them

Season average of the team's 1st through 5th against its 6th through 10th. A programme gets deeper or it does not, and the scoring five will not tell you which.

How they came in

A missing freshman year and a genuine late entry look identical from one athlete's own rows, so entry is reconciled against whether the school posted any freshmen that season at all.
