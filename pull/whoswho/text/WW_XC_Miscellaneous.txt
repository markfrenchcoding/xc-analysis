Hypothetical State Meet
Here's where it gets fun! Looking at the all-time top-70 best performers on the Lane Community College 5k
course at the Oregon State Championships, only three girls teams and four boys teams had at least five
runners ranked to allow team scores to be determined.
What follows is a hypothetical meet scoring those teams athletes!
51
46
Why are the 1976 and 1978 5k courses suspect? Consider this:
The seasons on either side of them (1975, 1977, 1979) average not
a single runner in the 14:00 minute range, 5 running in the 15:00s
and 49 in the 16:00s. The decade which follows show similar
performance ranges.
However, those numbers were abruptly improved in 1976 (2-
14:00s, 26-15:00s, 62-16:00s) and 1978 (10-14:00s, 73-15:00s, 54-
16:00s). Most athletes who ran consecutive States were unable to
come close to their standout mark of 1976 or 1978, and the track
times they ran following the questionable season are not consistent
with the standout XC times. Even the race management of the time
called the course accuracy into question.
As for the 2019 season where 50 girls ran in the 18:00-range and
19 in the 17:00 range, and 54 boys in the 15:00-range, the meet
director says the course was 70m-90m short.

students; 48 schools), and "Small" (450-5; 70+ schools), and there are also two separate lists (800 population cutoff) for
private schools. This latter list is shorter only because there are considerably fewer private schools in the state.
