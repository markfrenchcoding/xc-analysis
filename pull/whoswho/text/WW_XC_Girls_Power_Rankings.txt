All-Time Girls Cross Country Team Rankings
These teams have seen the most success historically, as defined by qualification for and achievement at the
Oregon State Championships. It accounts for the first State Meet in 1974 to 2025; prior to 1974 there were a
few years of an "Oregon Invitational" for girls, but those results are not incorporated into these rankings since it
was an open meet with no necessary District-level qualification standard.
This is a historic list, not limiting schools by their current classification placement but rather considering every
classification they have ever competed in.
Methodology: teams earn points for qualifying for state (10 points), for each team they beat at State (2 points), and for any top-four
finishes at State (1st-50 points, 2nd-30 points, 3rd-20 points, 4th-10 points; due to the occassional small number of complete teams in the
smallest classification prior to 1990, points are not awarded for a 4th place team finish unless the field of competition numbered at least
eight teams.)
