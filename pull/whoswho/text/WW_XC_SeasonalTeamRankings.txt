Top-10 Seasonal Cross Country Team Rankings - Girls 6A
This is the ranking of cross country teams based solely on their top seven runners Pr times each season.
This may differ from State meet results, by allowing a thid or fourth strong team from a District to be eligable for ranking consideration (whereas
they would not have qualified for or placed at State). It presumes all runners are health and running their best season Pr and does not account for a
coaches choice to rest a participant. Admittedly, the greatest subjective liberty in this ranking is that discounts thre impact of "fast" or"slow" race
courses, instead treating all with equal measure.
Source: Appreciation is extended to our friends at Athletic.Net, whose 'hypothetical meet' is the basis of this ranking.
Note: Since there was no central location by which teams could report their teams season Pr lists prior to 2005 (when Athletic.Net started), it is
impossible to reasonably reconstruct the top-10 rankings with any degree of accuracy prior to that season. State finishes would be the best source
for completing such older rankings, but even here 2-3 teams would not have made the Championships who should be in the top-ten, and Pr lists
could not be accounted for. Hence, rankings prior to 2005 do not exist.
