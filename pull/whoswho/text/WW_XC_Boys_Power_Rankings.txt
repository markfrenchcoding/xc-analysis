All-Time Boys Cross Country Team Rankings
These teams have seen the most success historically, as defined by qualification for and achievement at the Oregon State
Championships. It accounts for all State Championships from 2024 to 1960 (the first verfied year of a State-
qualification standard through District performance).
This is a historic list, not limiting schools by their current classification placement but rather considering every
classification they had once competed in.
Methodology: teams earn points for qualifying for state (10 points), for each team they beat at State (2 points), and for any top-four finishes at State
(1st-50 points, 2nd-30 points, 3rd-20 points, 4th-10 points; due to the occassional small number of complete teams in the smallest division, points
are not awarded for a 4th place team finish unless the field of competition numbered at least eight teams.)
