What it does

  1. Asks athletic.net for every Oregon high school and keeps the ones on an OSAA board.
  2. Walks each school's calendar for meets that have results.
  3. Pulls every 5,000 m division of those meets.
  4. Keeps each athlete's three fastest and each team's seven fastest, and refreshes the crests.
